# 🪔 Diwali Sales Analysis | Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project using Python to analyze Diwali sales data, understand customer purchasing behavior, identify top-performing customer segments, and generate actionable business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Diwali Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing patterns during the Diwali festive season.

Using Python and its data analysis libraries, this project explores customer demographics, purchasing behavior, product categories, marital status, occupations, and geographical distribution to identify valuable business insights.

The analysis helps understand which customer segments contribute most to sales and provides data-driven recommendations for marketing and product strategies.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Diwali sales data.
- Clean and preprocess raw customer data.
- Analyze purchasing behavior based on gender and age.
- Identify top-performing states and customer segments.
- Explore sales patterns across occupations and marital status.
- Identify the most popular product categories.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings

Based on the analysis:

- **Gender:** Female customers contribute more than 60% of sales volume and value.
- **Age Group:** Customers aged 26–35 show the highest engagement.
- **Geographical Distribution:** Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka are the top three states, contributing 45% of total orders.
- **Marital Status:** Married women represent an important customer segment with high purchasing power.
- **Occupation:** IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals are among the major customer segments.
- **Product Categories:** Food, Clothing, and Electronics are the best-selling categories.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following strategies were identified:

- Target married women professionals in the 26–35 age group.
- Focus marketing campaigns on Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka.
- Introduce family-oriented festive campaigns.
- Prioritize inventory planning for Food, Clothing, and Electronics.
- Create attractive family bundle offers during festive seasons.
- Explore premium product offerings for IT and Healthcare professionals.
